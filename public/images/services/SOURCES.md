Artwork and characters belong to Riot Games; no endorsement is implied. Card derivatives add a common vector design and real outlined Gmarket Sans Medium typography, separate from the Noto Sans KR hero banners. All card titles use x=88, baseline y=638, size=104; subtitles use x=88, baseline y=714, size=44. Canvas is 1280x800. No XYZ watermark or champion caption is included.

Editable SVGs: design/service-cards. Export script: scripts/build-service-cards.py (fonttools and sharp; FONT_PATH to GmarketSansTTFMedium.ttf).
